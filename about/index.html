About Us

Grill Master began as a humble street-side grill in 1995, founded by a passionate chef who believed that real flavor comes only from real fire. With a small charcoal pit and a handful of secret spice mixes passed down through generations, the restaurant quickly became a hidden gem for meat lovers.

As word spread, Grill Master transformed from a simple family shop into a thriving culinary destination. By the early 2000s, our dedication helped us win several regional awards.

As our reputation grew, Grill Master entered multiple international culinary competitions and proudly earned several awards—establishing ourselves globally.

Today, Grill Master is a worldwide brand known for excellence in charcoal grilling. We continue delivering bold flavors and warm hospitality across the globe.
